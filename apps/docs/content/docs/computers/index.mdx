A bot works on the computer running OpenMausBot through its shell and files there. MausBot gives it no screen tools of its own.

On a Lazurio Environment, bots also work with the programs on the Environment's screen through the Environment's Computer Use: the `computer` tools every agent on the Environment gets, with which a bot opens a program, clicks and types. They take turns on the screen with the other agents there, since it has one mouse and keyboard. Bots work in the Environment browser through `agent-browser` with their own session. Elsewhere they have no browser and no screen.

## Website sign-in

When a bot needs you to sign in to a website, it asks with a **Sign in needed** card instead of asking for a password in chat. You sign in yourself in the bot's tab of the Environment browser; **Done** resumes the bot. See [Website sign-in](/docs/security/permissions-and-secrets#website-sign-in).
