A bot works on the computer running OpenMausBot through its shell and files there. It has no screen tools of its own: it does not open desktop apps or click on this computer's screen.

On a Lazurio Environment, bots work in the Environment browser through `agent-browser` with their own session; elsewhere they have no browser.

## Website sign-in

When a bot needs you to sign in to a website, it asks with a **Sign in needed** card instead of asking for a password in chat. You sign in yourself in the bot's tab of the Environment browser; **Done** resumes the bot. See [Website sign-in](/docs/security/permissions-and-secrets#website-sign-in).
